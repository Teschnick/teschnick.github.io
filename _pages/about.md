I am currently a volunteer in the VVISE Lab at SFU with a specialisation in application development with Unity. 
My academic background startet with a Bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering which was completed in 2018 in Chemnitz University of Technology. Midway through the consecutive Master's degree I switched to working with XR technologies. 
Since graduating in 2022, I worked for 4 years in the department of process informatic and virtual product development as a research associate. There, I gained experience in developing digital twins with Unity and using AI models like Tesseract and MediaPipe for diverse XR applications. 
What I like most in software development is creating efficient systems and tinkering with frameworks to adapt them to my needs. AI tools are a good way to get into a new topic but I quickly reach a point where debugging a models output is more trouble than doing it myself. 
On this site, you can find my portfolio, sadly I can’t show most of my work of the past years, publications I wrote or contributed to, the teaching I did in my previous position and my CV.

Outside of work, I’m a big fantasy fan and like to play all kind of RPG, read books or manga and watch anime. To compensate all of that mental activity, I also do sports multiple times a week.
