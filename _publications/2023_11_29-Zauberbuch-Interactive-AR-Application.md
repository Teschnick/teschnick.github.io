Abstract
======
Projective Augmented Reality sees use in a variety of application areas to support users at certain tasks or to create better ways of visualization, especially where wearing special equipment would be unpractical or the number of users varies greatly. In this paper, the development of an interactive, projective AR-based book-prototype, which is being used in a public university library, and so far gathered user feedback, is described.
