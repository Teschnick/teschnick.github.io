Cultural Capital of Europe project
======

The "Zauberbuch" or magic book was developed as an interactive exhibit for Chemnitz University of Technology's library to celebrate Chemnitz as Cultural Capital of Europe. The thumbnail showed the final setup as it is still used in the library, which was published at [EuroXR 23](https://publications.vtt.fi/pdf/technology/2023/T422.pdf). 

The application was built with Unity and utilizes [Tesseract](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) for optical character recognition (OCR) to recognize page numbers and display content dynamically. By loading pictures and text data directly from files, the setup can be easily adapted to new exhibition themes.
