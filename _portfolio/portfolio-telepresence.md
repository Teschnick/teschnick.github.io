HMD Application
======

The overall setup consisted of two applications running on different computers and exchanging data via UDP. The HMD application took in the 360° video stream, rendered it on a sphere in Unity and overlayed a grid via a custom shader. The previous picture showed the HMD view of the lab with overlayed grid and is taken from the [corresponding publication](https://openaccess.cms-conferences.org/publications/book/978-1-964867-76-2/article/978-1-964867-76-2_25). You can also find more info there.


Control Application
======

The second application serfed multiple purposes: it established the connection with the AGV, enabling control via gamepad and controlled the whole user study procedure. The experimenter could control and monitor study progress and errors via a user interface and also control what was shown on the HMD. I am unfortunately not allowed to share more detailed information or screeshots of this side, as it was not published anywhere.
