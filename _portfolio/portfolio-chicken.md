Chicken Dinner or Biscuit Winner
======

Entry for the theme "Risk it for the biscuit". Was solo developed in roughly 30 hours with the use of freely available models for the characters, the cookies and features in the environment. If you want to try, you can follow [this link](https://teschnick.itch.io/chicken-dinner-or-biscuit-winner) to the itch.io site!

Description: As a well fed chicken you are not interested in chicken feed anymore. On a warm summer day you spot a more suited meal - freshly baked cookies. But beware! The intended cookie receiver will not tolerate cookie thieves. A round chicken makes for a nice dinner after all. Will the risk be worth it?
