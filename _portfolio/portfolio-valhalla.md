You and your men died a honourable death in combat. But the way to Valhalla is still long and a horde of draugr is in your way. Fight your way to the golden halls!

Was solo developed in a week with the use of freely available models for the characters, animations and soundfiles and AI. If you want to try, you can follow [this link](https://teschnick.itch.io/road-to-valhalla) to the itch.io site!

This game uses assets from the Unity Assetstore and other platforms:

	* Deep Space Skybox Pack
	* Norse Shield
	* Free PBR Viking Axe
	* Free Viking Folk Music Pack
	* Warrior viking with red hair and armor
	* Model and Animations from Mixamo
	* Soundeffects from pixabay


This game contains AI generated content in form of UI sprites, textures and code.
